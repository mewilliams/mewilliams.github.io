
## Oceanographic and Earth Science Data in Chile

### Tides

- [SHOA](http://www.shoa.cl/)
- [IOC Sea Level Monitoring Stations (Chile)](https://www.ioc-sealevelmonitoring.org/list.php?order=delay&dir=asc&operator=59)

### Waves

- [SHOA Wave Monitoring Network](http://www.shoa.cl/)
- [Atlas de Oleaje de Chile](https://oleaje.uv.cl/)
- [ERA5 Reanalysis (ECMWF)](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/dataset/ecmwf-reanalysis-v5)

### Meteorological Data

- https://climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl/application/informacion/buscadorEstaciones
- [Dirección General de Aguas (DGA)](https://dga.mop.gob.cl/)
- [CEAZAmet](https://www.ceazamet.cl/) (meteorological and oceanographic observations)

### Hydrologic Data

- [Dirección General de Aguas (DGA)](https://dga.mop.gob.cl/)
  - Streamflow gauges
  - Water rights
  - Groundwater wells

- [IFOP CHONOS](https://chonos.ifop.cl/)

### Oceanographic Buoys in Chile

- [SHOA](http://www.shoa.cl/)
