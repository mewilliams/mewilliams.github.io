My research focuses on coastal wetlands, estuaries, sediment transport, groundwater-surface water interactions, and the response of coastal systems to climate variability and change. --- ## Welcome I am an Assistant Professor at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research combines field observations, numerical modeling, remote sensing, and data analysis to understand how oceanic and watershed processes interact across coastal environments. Current research themes include: - Coastal wetlands and lagoonal estuaries - Surface water and groundwater interactions - Sediment transport and morphodynamics - Wave, tide, and storm impacts on coastal systems - Climate variability and change in coastal watersheds - Ocean renewable energy --- ## Research ### Coastal Wetlands as Integrators of Watershed and Ocean Dynamics Coastal wetlands are dynamic ecosystems located at the interface between land and sea.
